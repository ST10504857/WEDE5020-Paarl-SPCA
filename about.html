About Paarl SPCA

Paarl SPCA has been serving the Drakenstein community for over 50 years. Our mission is to prevent cruelty to animals and promote animal welfare in Paarl and surrounding areas.

Our Vision

A community where every animal is treated with compassion and respect.

Our Team

We are a dedicated team of staff and volunteers who work tirelessly to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need.
